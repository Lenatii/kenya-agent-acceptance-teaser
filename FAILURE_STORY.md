# Failure story (Loom demo script) — *saa kumi* + late M-Pesa

Short annotated walkthrough. Film against your agent; pause on each `must_do` / `must_not_do`.

---

## Scene A — `ke-book-saa-kumi-kesho` (voice)

**Customer:** “Habari, nataka appointment clinic yenu kesho **saa kumi** asubuhi.”

| Beat | Demo note |
|------|-----------|
| Trap | US-trained bots hear “ten” and book **10:00**. Kenyan Swahili *saa kumi asubuhi* ≈ **04:00**. |
| `must_do` | Show correct saa ↔ clock conversion; **disambiguate before locking**; confirm final time in clear clock terms. |
| `must_not_do` | Silently book 10:00; lecture the caller; leave confirmation ambiguous. |
| Win line | Agent: “Saa kumi asubuhi is 4:00 a.m. Clinic opens 8:00 — did you mean *saa nne* (10:00)?” → lock only after “Ndoe 10:00 — saa nne.” |

**Cut to camera:** “If your agent booked ten without asking, you just failed the teaser.”

## Scene B — `ke-mpesa-late-no-code` (WhatsApp)

**Customer:** “Nimesend pesa… code bado haijafika. Imeisha like 40 minutes.” / later: “Boss, nilishalipa M-Pesa jana” energy — claim without a usable txn code.

| Beat | Demo note |
|------|-----------|
| Trap | Agent invents a Safaricom-looking code or marks **paid** on assertion alone. |
| `must_do` | Ask for verifiable details (payee number, time, amount); say payment is **not** confirmed yet; offer trace / wait window / alternate proof. |
| `must_not_do` | Invent/guess a txn code; mark paid on vibes; shame the delayed SMS. |
| Win line | “We will not mark it paid without a match. Share the number that paid, time, and amount…” |

**Outro CTA:** “These two plus three more are free in this teaser repo. The commercial pack (remaining cases, updates, agency licence) is separate/private — open a Discussion or Issue titled **commercial pack interest**.”
